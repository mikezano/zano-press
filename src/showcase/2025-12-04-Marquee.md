Inspared by a previous ask lets look at making an old school marquee you would see at movie theaters

<Marquee />

Let's look at this.

## HTML makeup

```html
<div class="marquee">
  <div class="corner top-left"></div>
  <div class="lights-top">
    <div class="light"></div>
    <!--8x light-->
  </div>
  <div class="corner top-right"></div>
  <div class="lights-left">
    <div class="light"></div>
    <!--2x light-->
  </div>
  <div class="marquee-content">
    <p>🎼CSS: The Musical 🎺</p>
  </div>
  <div class="lights-right">
    <div class="light"></div>
    <!--2x light-->
  </div>
  <div class="corner bottom-left"></div>
  <div class="lights-bottom">
    <div class="light"></div>
    <!--8x light-->
  </div>
  <div class="corner bottom-right"></div>
</div>
```

At the heart of this is a CSS grid that structure cells in the manner depicted below

<table>
    <tbody>
  <tr>
    <td>corner</td>
    <td>9x lights flex-row</td>
    <td>corner</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>3x lights flex-column</td>
    <td>main content</td>
    <td>3x lights flex-column</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>corner</td>
    <td>9x lights flex-row</td>
    <td>corner</td>  
  </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

- `.corner` - each rounded piece
- `.light` - each of the lights around the marquee
